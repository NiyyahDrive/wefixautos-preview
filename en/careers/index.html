Open vacancy

Car mechanic (M/F)

As a car mechanic you work on a variety of vehicles and take on new technical challenges every day.

Location
Antwerp / Merksem
Hours
Full-time
Contract type
Permanent contract

Your tasks

  • Servicing: you carry out both minor and major services, from oil changes to replacing filters and checking essential fluids.
  • Diagnostics and repairs: you track down faults in mechanical and electronic systems and make sure everything works again.
  • Mechanical repairs: you repair or replace parts such as brakes and suspension, with an eye for safety and quality.
  • Tyres: you are responsible for fitting, removing, balancing and repairing tyres.
  • Technical work: you work on components such as lighting, batteries, starter motors and on-board electronics.

Who are we looking for?

  • You have experience as a car mechanic or a strong technical foundation.
  • You work accurately and have an eye for detail.
  • You can work independently, but also function well in a team.
  • You are motivated and open to learning all the time.
  • A qualification in automotive technology is a plus, but not a requirement.

What do we offer?

  • Fair and attractive pay.
  • A close-knit team where teamwork and a good working atmosphere are central.
  • Opportunities to progress within the company.
  • In-house guidance and support where needed.
