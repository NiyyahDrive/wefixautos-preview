Servicing

Servicing for every make.

Minor and major services for all car makes. Timely servicing keeps your car safe, helps avoid unexpected repairs and extends the life of your car.

What we do

Minor or major service.

Which service your car needs depends on the type of car. Usually minor and major services alternate.

  • Minor service

    Replacing and checking the most important parts: oil change, a new oil filter, a check of brakes, tyres and suspension, topping up fluids and a general check of the car.

  • Major service

    Goes a step further. Depending on the type of car we also replace the air filter, the cabin filter, the fuel filter (diesel cars) or the spark plugs (petrol cars).

  • Brakes, tyres and suspension

    At every service we check the parts that determine your safety.

  • Fluids and general check

    We top up the fluids and go over the car, so you know what condition it is in.

When to come by

When is it time for a service?

A service is generally carried out every 15,000 to 30,000 kilometres. These are the moments to plan one.

  • Your car has driven 15,000 to 30,000 kilometres since the last service
  • The prescribed service interval for your car has been reached
  • The last service was a year ago, even if you drive little: engine oil, filters and fluids age
  • You want to avoid unexpected repairs

Our approach

How your service goes.

  1. Tailored quote

    You give us the type of car, the year of manufacture and the fuel. The price depends on make, model and the type of service; the chassis number (VIN) helps us look that up correctly.

  2. Appointment

    You call or e-mail your branch to fix a time.

  3. Service and check

    We carry out the minor or major service and check your car.

  4. We consult you first about extra work

    If we find necessary repairs, we contact you first, discuss the situation and give a clear price indication. Extra repairs are only done after your approval.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Do you service all makes?

Yes, at WeFix Auto's we carry out servicing for all car makes.

How much does a service for my car cost?

The price of a service depends on the make, model and type of service needed. Request a quote and we will send you an accurate quote tailored to your vehicle.

Can I make an appointment for a service?

Yes, you can contact us by phone or by e-mail. You can also request a no-obligation quote.

My car is driven very little. Does it still need a service every year?

Yes, even with low mileage. Engine oil, filters and other fluids age over time. So have your car checked annually or according to the prescribed service interval.

How often does my car need a service?

A service is generally carried out every 15,000 to 30,000 kilometres. Usually minor and major services are carried out alternately to keep your car in optimal condition.

What if extra costs are discovered during the service?

When we find necessary repairs during the service, we always contact you first. We discuss the situation and give a clear price indication. Extra repairs are only carried out after your approval.
