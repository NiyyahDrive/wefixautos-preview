Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Is your question not listed? Ask it via the form or call a branch.

Do you service all makes?

Yes, at WeFix Auto's we carry out servicing for all car makes.

How much does a service for my car cost?

The price of a service depends on the make, model and type of service needed. Fill in the quote form and we will send you an accurate quote tailored to your vehicle.

Do I need an appointment for tyre service?

No, you do not always need an appointment in advance for our tyre service. You can contact us by phone or drop in at one of our branches.

Can you take my car to the roadworthiness test?

Yes. We can present your car at the test centre. That way you do not have to plan an appointment or a visit to the test centre yourself.

Are repairs carried out automatically when a problem is found?

No. When we find defects during the check, we contact you first. Repairs are only carried out after your approval.

What are lease tyres?

Lease tyres are quality used tyres that still have sufficient tread. They are checked before being fitted again and are an affordable alternative for anyone looking for reliable tyres at a lower price.

My car is driven very little. Does it still need a service every year?

Yes, even with low mileage. Engine oil, filters and other fluids age over time. So have your car checked annually or according to the prescribed service interval.
