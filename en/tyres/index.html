What we do

Everything to do with your tyres.

Our technicians look after the safety and comfort of your vehicle and help you quickly with all your tyre work.

  • Replacing tyres

    We supply and fit both new tyres and quality lease tyres, for all vehicle makes.

  • Swapping tyres

    Do you already have a set of tyres yourself? Then we fit and swap them professionally.

  • Seasonal change

    Time for summer or winter tyres? We take care of a quick and correct change, so you can get back on the road safely.

  • Balancing tyres

    Balanced tyres give a more comfortable drive, less wear and more stable road holding.

  • Repairing tyres

    Not every puncture means the tyre has to be replaced. Depending on the damage, we repair your tyre with a plug or with an internal repair.

  • Replacing a valve

    A faulty valve can lead to pressure loss. We check the valves and replace them when they are worn.

When to come by

When to come for tyre service?

Not sure about the condition of your tyres? We check them and advise you on the best solution.

  • The tread is too worn
  • The tyres are damaged or show signs of ageing
  • Your steering wheel vibrates, especially at high speeds: that can point to unbalanced tyres
  • A tyre is punctured or losing pressure
  • It gets colder than 7 °C (winter tyres) or the temperature rises again (summer tyres)

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

What are lease tyres?

Lease tyres are quality used tyres that still have sufficient tread. They are checked before being fitted again and are an affordable alternative for anyone looking for reliable tyres at a lower price.

When should I change my summer or winter tyres?

Winter tyres perform better at temperatures below 7 °C. When the temperature rises again, it is recommended to switch back to summer tyres. We are happy to help you with your seasonal change.

Do I need an appointment for tyre service?

No, you do not always need an appointment in advance for our tyre service. You can contact us by phone or drop in at one of our branches.

Why does my steering wheel vibrate while driving?

Vibrations in the steering wheel, especially at high speeds, can point to unbalanced tyres. Balancing corrects small differences in weight, so the wheels turn evenly. That reduces vibrations, improves driving comfort and helps prevent uneven wear.

When should I replace my tyres?

When the tread is too worn, the tyres are damaged or show signs of ageing. Not sure? We check your tyres and advise you on the best solution.

How much does it cost to replace or repair my tyres?

The cost depends on the tyres you want, the tyre size and the work needed. Contact us by phone or by e-mail, or request a quote. We will inform you about the options and the associated costs.

Can a puncture always be repaired?

No. It depends on the location, size and nature of the damage and on the general condition of the tyre. We check the tyre and assess whether a safe repair is possible or replacement is necessary.
