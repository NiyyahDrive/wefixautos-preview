What we do

Belt or chain: we check it.

Whether your car has a timing belt or a timing chain differs per make, model and engine.

  • Replacing the timing belt

    A timing belt wears and must be replaced according to the service schedule. If it breaks while driving, that can lead to serious engine damage and high repair costs.

  • Replacing the timing chain

    A timing chain generally lasts longer, but is not completely maintenance-free. Wear, a stretched chain or a faulty chain tensioner can cause problems. We replace the chain together with the necessary parts.

  • Checking the timing system

    Not sure whether the timing belt or chain is due for replacement? Our technicians check it and advise you on the right solution.

When to come by

Signs of a worn timing belt or chain.

Do you notice one or more of these signs? Then have your car checked to avoid greater damage.

  • A rattling or squealing noise from the engine
  • The car is hard to start
  • The warning light is on
  • The engine loses power
  • The engine runs unevenly

Our approach

Properly informed, properly planned.

  1. Get in touch beforehand

    Call or e-mail us and give us your chassis number. We check which system your car has.

  2. Check and advice

    We check the timing belt or chain. The replacement interval differs per car make and model; we are happy to look that up for your vehicle.

  3. Quote and planning

    We inform you correctly about the replacement and the planning. How long the work takes depends on make, model and engine.

  4. Replacement

    We replace the belt or chain together with the necessary parts, so your engine runs reliably.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

How do I know whether my car has a timing belt or a timing chain?

This differs per make, model and engine. Feel free to contact us or give us your chassis number. We check which system your car has.

What happens if a timing belt breaks?

A broken timing belt can cause serious engine damage, because the engine parts no longer work together correctly. So make sure it is replaced in time.

How long does it take to replace a timing belt or timing chain?

This depends on the car make, the model and the engine. Feel free to contact us for more information.

When does a timing belt need to be replaced?

The replacement interval differs per car make and model. Always consult the manufacturer's guidelines: they prescribe when the belt must be replaced, based on a number of kilometres or the age of the car. Not sure? We are happy to check this for your vehicle.

Do I need an appointment to have my timing belt or timing chain replaced?

We recommend contacting us beforehand by phone or by e-mail. That way we can inform you correctly about the replacement and the planning.

Why is my engine making a rattling noise?

A rattling noise can have several causes, including a worn or stretched timing chain. Have your car checked in time to prevent further wear or serious engine damage.
