What we do

From diagnosis to repair.

Our technicians work on mechanical and electronic systems, with an eye for safety and quality.

  • Diagnosis

    We track down faults in mechanical and electronic systems and find the cause.

  • Mechanical repairs

    We repair or replace parts such as brakes and suspension.

  • Electrical components

    We also work on lighting, batteries, starter motors and on-board electronics.

  • Timing belt and air conditioning

    You can also come to us for a timing belt or timing chain and for the repair of an air-conditioning leak.

When to come by

When should you have your car checked?

Do not wait too long with a car that responds differently from what you are used to. A timely check helps avoid greater damage.

  • A warning light is on
  • The car is hard to start
  • You hear an unusual noise, such as rattling, squealing or grinding
  • You feel vibrations while driving or braking
  • The engine loses power or runs unevenly

Our approach

No repair without consulting you.

  1. You describe the problem

    Call or e-mail your branch, or request a quote with the type of car and the year of manufacture.

  2. We find the cause

    We check your car and work out what is wrong.

  3. First an explanation

    When we find defects, we contact you first and discuss the solution.

  4. Repair after your approval

    Repairs are only carried out after your approval.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Do you repair all car makes?

Yes. At WeFix Auto's you can come for servicing, tyres and repairs for all car makes.

Are repairs carried out automatically if a problem is found?

No. When we find defects during a check, we contact you first. Repairs are only carried out after your approval.

What does a repair cost?

That depends on the vehicle, the parts required and the work that has to be carried out. That is why we make a quote tailored to your vehicle.

How do I make an appointment?

Contact your branch by phone or by e-mail, or request a quote. That way we can plan a suitable time.
