Why used tyres

Affordable, without compromising on safety.

Affordable tyres, but never at the expense of safety or honesty.

  • Economical alternative

    Used tyres are quality second-hand tyres with sufficient tread: an economical alternative to new tyres.

  • Carefully checked

    All used tyres are checked for wear, tread and general condition before they are fitted again.

  • Often available immediately

    Thanks to the extensive stock, the right tyre is often available immediately and we can help you quickly.

  • A more sustainable choice

    Choosing quality used tyres gives tyres a second life.

WeFix Tyre Check

What we check with every fitting.

When fitting, we check six points as standard, so you take to the road safely and reliably.

  1. Tyre pressure
  2. Wear
  3. Visible damage
  4. General condition
  5. Correct fitting
  6. Combination of vehicle and tyre

How it works

On different tyres in four steps.

  1. You give us your tyre size

    Fill in the short form. Do not know the size? Then the make, model and year of manufacture of your car are enough.

  2. We check the stock

    The branch checks which tyres are suitable for your car and lets you know the options.

  3. You come by

    For our tyre service you do not always need an appointment in advance. Give us a call or drop in.

  4. Fitting and balancing

    We fit the tyres professionally, balance them and replace the valves if necessary.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

What is the difference between used tyres and new tyres?

Used tyres are quality second-hand tyres with sufficient tread. They are an economical alternative to new tyres.

How do I know which tyre size I need?

You will find the tyre size on the sidewall of your current tyre or in the owner's manual of your car. Not sure? Feel free to contact us.

Are used tyres safe?

Yes. Our used tyres are carefully checked for wear and general condition before they are fitted again.

Do I need to make an appointment?

No, you do not always need an appointment in advance for our tyre service. You can contact us by phone or drop in at one of our branches.

Can you fit the tyres as well?

Yes. We can professionally fit and balance the used tyres and, if necessary, replace the valves.
