Brakes

Brakes you can trust.

Checking, replacing and repairing your brakes. Are they squealing, responding less firmly, or do you feel vibrations when braking? Then have them checked in time.

What we do

Every part of your braking system.

Brakes that work well are essential for your safety. We check what is worn and replace what is needed.

  • Replacing brake pads

    Brake pads wear and must be replaced in time. Worn brake pads can cause reduced braking performance and damage to the brake discs.

  • Replacing brake discs

    Brake discs work together with the brake pads and are crucial for braking safely. Worn brake discs cause vibrations, noises and reduced braking performance.

  • Replacing calipers

    A faulty or seized caliper can cause uneven wear or less braking power, or make the car pull to one side when braking.

  • Checking and replacing brake fluid

    Over time the quality of the brake fluid can decline, which can affect how the braking system works.

When to come by

Recognise the signs.

Do you notice one of these signs? Have your brakes checked in time to prevent further wear and possible safety risks.

  • Your brakes squeal
  • The brakes respond less firmly or the braking distance gets longer
  • You feel vibrations in the steering wheel or in the car when braking
  • You hear a grinding noise when braking
  • The car pulls to one side when braking

Our approach

First find out what is wrong.

  1. Check

    We check your car to find the cause. Other parts of the braking system or the suspension can also play a role.

  2. Advice and quote

    We advise whether replacement is needed. The cost depends on the vehicle, the parts required and the work.

  3. Replace or repair

    We replace or repair the parts that are due for replacement, so you can brake safely again.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Why does my car vibrate when braking?

Vibrations in the steering wheel or the car when braking can be caused by, among other things, worn or warped brake discs. Other parts of the braking system or the suspension can also play a role. We check your car to find the cause.

When do brake discs need to be replaced?

Brake discs need to be replaced when they are too thin, damaged or unevenly worn. We check the condition of your brake discs and advise whether replacement is needed.

What does it cost to replace or repair my brakes?

The cost depends on the vehicle, the parts required and the work that has to be carried out. Request a quote or contact us by phone or by e-mail.

Can I keep driving if my brakes squeal?

Squealing brakes can point to wear, but do not always mean there is a serious problem. Because brakes that work well are essential for your safety, we recommend having your brakes checked in time.

How do I know whether my brake pads are worn?

Worn brake pads can cause grinding noises, a longer braking distance or less braking power. Not sure? Have your brakes checked, so we can see whether replacement is needed.
